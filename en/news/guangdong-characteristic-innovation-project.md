We are pleased to announce that the lab's application to the 2026 Guangdong University Research Platforms and Projects Program has been approved as a Characteristic Innovation Class project.

- **Project title**: Geometric Phase Control Methods for High-Fidelity Superconducting Quantum Gates and Experimental Verification
- **Project No.**: 2026KTSCX106

The project will develop geometric phase control methods for high-fidelity superconducting quantum gates, exploit the intrinsic robustness of geometric phases against certain control errors, and verify the methods experimentally on superconducting quantum processors, supporting high-fidelity gate operations toward scalable superconducting quantum computing.

We are pleased to announce that the lab's application for the General Program of the National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) has been approved.

- **Project title**: Dark-State-Protected High-Fidelity Readout of Superconducting Qubits without Parametric Amplification
- **Grant No.**: 12674609
- **Period**: January 2027 – December 2030

The project aims to develop readout schemes based on dark-state protection, achieving high-fidelity readout of superconducting qubits without relying on parametric amplifiers, and to explore a viable technical path toward scaling up superconducting quantum computing.

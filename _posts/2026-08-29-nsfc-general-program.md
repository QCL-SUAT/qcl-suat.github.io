We are pleased to announce that the application for the General Program of the National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC), led by Dr. Shuoming An, has been approved.

- **Project title**: Dark-State-Protected High-Fidelity Readout of Superconducting Qubits without Parametric Amplification
- **Grant No.**: 12674609
- **PI**: Prof. Shuoming An
- **Period**: January 2027 – December 2030

The project aims to develop readout schemes based on dark-state protection, achieving high-fidelity readout of superconducting qubits without relying on parametric amplifiers.

课题组负责人安硕明申请的国家自然科学基金**面上项目**获批立项！

- **项目名称**：基于暗态保护的无参量放大超导量子比特高保真读出
- **项目批准号**：12674609
- **项目负责人**：安硕明
- **执行期限**：2027 年 1 月 – 2030 年 12 月

本项目拟基于暗态保护原理设计超导量子比特的读出方案，在不依赖参量放大器的情况下实现高保真读出，为超导量子计算的大规模扩展提供关键技术支持。
